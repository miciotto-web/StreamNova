HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Eat food to grow. Avoid walls and your own tail. Get the highest score!

CONTROLS

↑↓←→ or WASD — Move
Space — Pause / Resume
R — Restart
M — Mute / Unmute
Esc — Back to menu

MOBILE

Swipe to move. Tap buttons for pause, restart, menu.

MODES

Classic — Standard snake gameplay. Fill the grid to win!
Arcade — Score as high as possible before time runs out. Eat food for bonus time!
Survival — How long can you survive? Difficulty increases over time.

DIFFICULTY

Easy — Slower, more forgiving.
Normal — Balanced challenge.
Hard — Fast and aggressive.

POWER-UPS

Collect glowing icons that appear on the grid:
⚡ Speed — Faster movement.
❄ Slow — Slower movement.
×2 Score×2 — Double points.
👻 Ghost — Pass through yourself.
🧲 Magnet — Attract food.

SPECIAL FOOD

🟡 Gold — Worth 30 points.
🔵 Bonus — Worth 50 points.
☠ Poison — Loses 5 points and breaks combo.

COMBO

Eat food quickly to build combo. Higher combo = higher multiplier. Timer runs out = combo lost!
